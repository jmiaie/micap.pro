## Frontmatter

A note carries a title, a description, a publication date, an optional updated date, a pillar, and a byline. The byline is Micap LLC or a practice desk, such as Micap Real Estate Desk. An optional image path can replace the generated card.

## Body

The body is the note. It can use Markdown or MDX. It should describe a way of looking at a decision. It should not invent a client, a result, or a figure.

## What this sample is not

This sample is not a case study. It does not describe an engagement, and it does not report a result. The practice page is where the work is described.
